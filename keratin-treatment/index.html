Keratin Treatment in Poughkeepsie, NY

Frizz, handled for a season.

Keratin smoothing treatments that cut blow-dry time in half and keep humidity from having an opinion about your hair — from a 30-day reset to a four-month relaxing treatment. The consultation is complimentary.

Book Keratin Smoothing

Choosing your smoothing treatment

Keratin Smoothing Treatment, 30-day ($150+): a frizz-taming reset that lasts about a month — perfect before a trip, a wedding or summer. Keratin Relaxing Treatment, 4-month ($350+): deep smoothing that holds for a season and makes daily styling almost unnecessary. Magic Sleek ($300+): a salon-grade straightening system for lasting sleekness. B Blowout ($250+): a professional smoothing blowout for long-wear frizz control with more movement left in.

A keratin treatment smooths the cuticle and seals in a protein layer; it doesn't bleach or permanently change your curl pattern, and results soften gradually rather than growing out with a line. The keratin consultation is complimentary and the right place to talk through texture, color history and the finish you want.

Before and after

Arrive with clean, product-free hair if you can. Afterward, use sulfate-free shampoo and skip tight ponytails for the first days as your stylist advises — small habits that stretch the result. Color can usually be scheduled around a keratin treatment; ask at the consultation and we'll sequence the two.

Hudson Valley humidity, solved

Our stylists do smoothing treatments year-round in Poughkeepsie, from June humidity to winter static. Open seven days, free parking, and a full salon menu if you want a cut or gloss in the same visit.

Questions

Good to know

How long does a keratin treatment last?

The 30-day treatment lasts about a month; the relaxing treatment holds around four months. Results fade gradually, not with a regrowth line.

Will it make my hair straight?

It smooths and reduces frizz and volume; how straight depends on the treatment you choose and your texture. Magic Sleek is the most straightening option.

Can I color my hair and get keratin?

Usually yes — your stylist will sequence them at the consultation.

How long is the appointment?

Plan two to three hours depending on length and density.

Is the consultation free?

Yes — keratin consultations are complimentary.
