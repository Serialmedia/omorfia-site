Questions

Good to know

How do you sanitize tools?

Implements are sterilized between every guest and single-use items are exactly that — single use.

How long does gel last?

Two to three weeks for most guests, depending on your nails and how you use your hands.

Can I pair nails with another service?

Absolutely — nails and a massage or facial is the most common pairing on our books.
