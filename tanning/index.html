Questions

Good to know

When should I tan before an event?

One to two days before is the sweet spot — the color settles and any guide bronzer washes away.

How do I make it last?

Exfoliate before your session, then moisturize daily and keep long hot showers to a minimum.

Will it look natural?

That's the entire job. We match depth of color to your skin tone so it reads sun, not spray.
