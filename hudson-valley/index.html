Salon & Day Spa Serving the Hudson Valley

One address. The whole valley.

Omorfia sits on Dutchess Turnpike (Route 44) in Poughkeepsie — the former Marlene Weber Day Spa building, sharing the Adams Fairacre Farms lot — within a twenty-five-minute drive of most of Dutchess and eastern Ulster County. Here’s how guests from each town find us, and what they tend to book.

By town

Drive times & directions

Hyde Park

about 10 minutes north on Route 9

Hyde Park guests come down Route 9 for Sunday massages and color — we're the closest full day spa south of the Vanderbilt and FDR sites.

Wappingers Falls

about 15 minutes south via Route 9 or the Taconic

Wappingers and Hopewell guests tend to book the long version: a 90-minute massage, a facial, nails — a package makes the drive worth it.

LaGrange

about 10 minutes east on Route 44 / Route 55

LaGrangeville and Freedom Plains are straight down Dutchess Turnpike — the easiest drive on this list.

Pleasant Valley

about 10 minutes east on Route 44

Route 44 brings Pleasant Valley and Salt Point right to our lot — the Adams Fairacre Farms lot, to be exact.

Fishkill

about 20 minutes south on Route 9

Fishkill and Beacon guests book us for the things a chain doesn't do: Japanese head spa, balayage, bridal parties, full spa days.

Hopewell Junction

about 20 minutes via the Taconic

Taconic to Route 55 to Route 44 — twenty minutes, free parking, and a seven-day schedule that fits around commuting.

Rhinebeck

about 25 minutes north on Route 9

Rhinebeck and Red Hook guests come for salon-and-spa days without resort pricing, and for Sunday appointments.

Beacon

about 25 minutes south

A spa day in Poughkeepsie pairs well with a Beacon weekend — book the For Two package and make an afternoon of it.

New Paltz

about 20 minutes across the Mid-Hudson Bridge

New Paltz, Highland and Gardiner guests cross the bridge for hair color, lashes and massage — and for a parking lot.

Highland

about 15 minutes across the bridge

Highland and Marlboro are a bridge away: over the Mid-Hudson, up Route 44, and you're here.

Drive times are typical off-peak estimates. Free parking in our lot at 751 Dutchess Turnpike, Poughkeepsie, NY 12603 — open in Google Maps.

Questions

Good to know

Where exactly is Omorfia?

751 Dutchess Turnpike (Route 44), Poughkeepsie, NY 12603 — the grey and white former Marlene Weber building sharing the Adams Fairacre Farms parking lot, minutes from Arlington and Vassar College.

Is there parking?

Yes — free parking in the lot, no garage, no meters.

Do you serve clients outside Poughkeepsie?

Most of our guests drive in from across Dutchess and Ulster counties — Hyde Park, Wappingers Falls, LaGrange, Pleasant Valley, Fishkill, Hopewell Junction, Rhinebeck, Beacon, New Paltz and Highland among them.

Do you offer on-location bridal services?

Ask during your bridal consultation — depending on the date, venue and party size we'll recommend in-salon or discuss on-location options.

Are you open weekends?

Seven days a week, 9 AM to 8 PM, including Sundays.
