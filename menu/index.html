Why do some prices say 'starting at'?

Length, density, color history and service time change how much product and time a service takes. You'll have an exact quote before the service begins.

Do members get a discount?

Yes — each club includes its monthly service at member pricing, plus an unlimited 10% off all other salon and spa services and special product pricing. Month to month, no contract.

Can I book several services in one visit?

Yes — that's the point of a day spa. Stack hair, nails, massage and a facial into one appointment, or choose a spa package and save over à la carte.

When the book allows, gladly — booking online or calling (845) 694-4952 is the only way to guarantee a time.
