Privacy Policy

Your privacy.

Last updated November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Omorfia Salon and Suites Poughkeepsie LLC, doing business as Omorfia Salon & Day Spa (“Omorfia,” “we,” “us”), collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, book an appointment, buy a gift card or membership, or receive messages from us.

Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, mobile phone number, email address, birthday (optional), appointment history, service preferences and notes, and any information you share on intake or consultation forms.

Payment information: card payments are processed by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.

Website information: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited and general location, collected through cookies and similar tools that help the site and our booking widget work.

How we use your information

We use your information to book, confirm, remind you of and manage appointments; process payments, gift cards and memberships; respond to your questions; keep service records so your provider can give you the right care; send you offers if you have opted in; and run, secure and improve our business and website.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we may send appointment confirmations, reminders, waitlist and schedule updates, and, only if you separately opt in, promotional messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers that deliver messages on our behalf (such as our booking software and its messaging provider).

Email

We send appointment emails and receipts. Marketing emails go only to guests who have opted in, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our booking and point-of-sale software, payment processor, messaging and email providers, and website host, and only as needed for them to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our guests, staff and business.

How we protect and keep information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. We keep it for as long as needed to provide services, keep accurate business records and meet legal requirements. If a breach of computerized personal information occurs, we will notify affected guests as required by New York law.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or correct your information, stop marketing texts (reply STOP) or emails (use the unsubscribe link), or delete your client profile where we are not required to keep it. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information online. Services for minors are booked by a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
