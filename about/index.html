About Omorfia · Poughkeepsie’s Salon & Day Spa · Est. 2023

Where Beauty Meets Relaxation

The name Omorfia comes from the Greek word for beauty — and it’s this timeless pursuit that inspires everything we do. Founded with a vision to create a sanctuary where artistry, wellness, and luxury converge, Omorfia has become a beloved destination for those who seek more than just a service.

Our founders envisioned a place where clients could escape the pace of everyday life and step into a world of calm, beauty, and rejuvenation. That vision found its home in the former Marlene Weber Day Spa building on Dutchess Turnpike — a Poughkeepsie spa landmark for decades, now reimagined under new, local ownership. If you knew Marlene Weber, you know the address; Omorfia is what it has become. Today, it lives on through our dedicated team of artisans who treat every client as a canvas worthy of their finest work.

From our carefully curated product lines to our serene interiors, every element of Omorfia is designed to delight the senses and nurture the soul.

Locally owned

Poughkeepsie, by choice

Omorfia is founder-owned and operated on Dutchess Turnpike — not a franchise, not a chain. The standards come from ownership walking the floor: hand-picked providers, superior product lines, and a front desk that welcomes you like a guest in our home. Meet the team →

The family

Part of Kyriacou Beauty Group

Omorfia is the flagship day spa of Kyriacou Beauty Group, a Hudson Valley beauty and wellness family that also includes OMO Med Spa for medical aesthetics and Kyriacou Cosmetics. One standard, three doors.

Questions

Good to know

What does Omorfia mean?

Omorfia (ομορφιά) is the Greek word for beauty — the name reflects the family's Greek roots and the standard the salon is built around.

Is Omorfia the old Marlene Weber Day Spa?

Omorfia occupies the former Marlene Weber Day Spa building at 751 Dutchess Turnpike in Poughkeepsie. Marlene Weber closed; Omorfia opened in 2023 under new, local ownership with its own team and a full salon and spa menu.

Who owns Omorfia?

Omorfia is founder-owned by the Kyriacou family and is the flagship day spa of Kyriacou Beauty Group, which also operates OMO Med Spa and Kyriacou Cosmetics.

Is Omorfia a chain?

No — it's a single, locally owned salon and day spa in Poughkeepsie, NY.
