Questions

Good to know

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes — Omorfia is open seven days a week, including Sundays and evenings, so a massage, facial or blowout fits around your week instead of the other way around.

Where is Omorfia Salon & Day Spa located?

We're at 751 Dutchess Turnpike (Route 44), Poughkeepsie, NY 12603 — the former Marlene Weber building, the grey and white building sharing a parking lot with Adams Fairacre Farms, minutes from Arlington and Vassar College.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking online is the best way to guarantee your time and provider, but call the front desk at (845) 694-4952 and we'll fit you in whenever we can.

Can I book multiple services in one visit?

Yes — that's the point of a day spa. Stack hair, nails, massage and a facial into one appointment, or let us design the day for you with a package.

Is Omorfia the former Marlene Weber Day Spa?

Yes — Omorfia opened in July 2024 in the former Marlene Weber Day Spa building at 751 Dutchess Turnpike, under new, local ownership. Same landmark location, a new team and a full salon and spa menu.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes — book two massages for the same time and you'll be side by side, or choose the For Two spa package, which pairs massages, facials and pedicures for two guests.
