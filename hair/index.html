Questions

Good to know

How do I choose a stylist?

Tell us what you're looking for when you book — the front desk matches new guests with the stylist whose strengths fit the service.

Why are some prices 'starting points'?

Length, density and color history change how much product and time a service takes; your stylist confirms the exact quote before starting.

Do you do consultations?

Yes — haircut, balayage, vivid, keratin and extension consultations are all complimentary.
