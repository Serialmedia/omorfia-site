Start Your Bridal Booking

For the bride

Every bridal booking starts with a trial: we lock the look, the products, and the exact timing before the day itself. On the morning, your only job is to sit still and be celebrated.

Questions

Good to know

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you have a date — popular Hudson Valley wedding weekends fill months out, and your trial should happen four to eight weeks before the day.

Can you handle large bridal parties?

Yes — with hair, makeup and nail teams under one roof we sequence large parties without the bottleneck of a single traveling artist.

Do you offer on-site services?

Ask during your consultation — depending on the date, venue and party size we'll recommend in-salon or discuss on-location options.

What about grooms and guests?

Cuts, styling, and a pre-wedding glow-up for anyone in the party — everyone's welcome on the timeline.
