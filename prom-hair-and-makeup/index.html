Prom Hair & Makeup in Poughkeepsie, NY

The night deserves the full treatment.

Updos, waves, half-up styles, camera-ready makeup, lashes, nails and a sunless glow — prom gets the bridal treatment at Omorfia, with the whole friend group styled on one timeline under one roof.

Book Prom

Prom, the way we do it

Book hair and makeup back to back and your look is finished in a single sitting: a full updo ($115+ cash / $119.03+ credit) or half-up style ($70+ cash / $72.45+ credit), hot-tool curls or waves ($30+ cash / $31.05+ credit), then full-face makeup ($100 cash / $103.50 credit) with strip lashes ($15 cash / $15.53 credit) or clusters ($40 cash / $41.40 credit) for drama that lasts past the last song. A bang trim or toner the week before keeps everything crisp.

Bring photos of the dress and the look you love. Our stylists and makeup artists work with your hair type and your face, not a one-size template.

Nails, glow and the group

Gel manicures ($45 cash / $46.58 credit) and pedicures survive the night. A Show Stopper spray tan ($40 cash / $41.40 credit upper body / $80 cash / $82.80 credit full) booked one to two days before reads sun-kissed in photos. And because Omorfia is a full salon and day spa, your friends can all book the same afternoon and get ready together.

Prom weekends in Dutchess County fill fast — book as soon as you have the date, and ask about group timelines for parties of four or more.

Parents: we see you too

A blowout, a facial or a massage while the getting-ready happens is a very reasonable use of the afternoon.

Questions

Good to know

How far ahead should I book for prom?

As soon as you have the date — prom Saturdays in April, May and June book out weeks ahead.

Can my friends and I get ready together?

Yes. Tell us the group size when you book and we'll schedule hair, makeup and nails on one timeline.

Should I wash my hair before an updo?

Day-old, product-free hair usually holds an updo best; your stylist will tell you what works for your hair when you book.

Do you do a trial for prom?

Trials are available for hair or makeup if you want to lock the look — ask when booking.

When should I get my spray tan?

One to two days before, after any waxing, so the color settles.
