Questions

Good to know

How often should I get a facial?

Every four to six weeks matches your skin's natural cycle; your esthetician will recommend a cadence for your goals.

Will my skin be red afterward?

A signature facial leaves most guests glowing, not red. Stronger treatments can bring brief redness — we'll tell you exactly what to expect before we start.

What about medical aesthetics?

For injectables and medical-grade treatments, our sister practice OMO Med Spa has you covered — ask the front desk.
