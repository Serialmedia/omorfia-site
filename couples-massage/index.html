Couples Massage in Poughkeepsie, NY

Side by side. Phones off.

Two therapists, two tables, the same quiet room — a couples massage at Omorfia is the simplest way to spend an hour together that neither of you has to plan. Book 60, 90 or 120 minutes and choose Swedish, deep tissue or hot stone when you arrive.

Book for Two

How a couples massage works here

Book two massages for the same start time and tell the front desk it's a couples booking — we pair you with two therapists and set you up side by side. Each of you chooses your own style and pressure once you're on the table, so one of you can go deep tissue while the other melts into Swedish. You pay for time, not type: the same price for any style.

Hot stones, amethyst crystals, Reiki or aromatherapy can be added to either table. Arrive a few minutes early, hydrate, and leave the rest to us.

Make it a whole afternoon

The For Two spa package ($550, a $670 value) is our most-booked couples experience: two 60-minute massages, two European facials and two spa pedicures, about three and a half hours together for $275 a person. It's the anniversary, birthday, mother-and-daughter or best-friend plan that requires zero decisions.

Couples massage is also our most-gifted service — a gift card loaded for two massages or the For Two package arrives by email in a minute.

Poughkeepsie's day spa for two

We're on Dutchess Turnpike with free parking, minutes from Arlington, Vassar and the Taconic, and open seven days a week including Sundays and evenings — so a couples massage fits a weekend, a date night or a day off without rearranging anything.

Questions

Good to know

Do we have to pick the same massage?

No — each of you chooses your own style and pressure with your therapist. Same price for Swedish, deep tissue or hot stone.

How do I book a couples massage?

Book two massages for the same time online, or call (845) 694-4952 and say it's for a couple — we'll pair the therapists and the room.

Can we do more than a massage?

Yes — the For Two package adds facials and pedicures, or the front desk can build a custom day for two.

Is a couples massage a good gift?

It's our most-gifted service. Gift cards work for any amount, a specific service or the For Two package.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes — seven days a week, 9 AM to 8 PM.
