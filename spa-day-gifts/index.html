How it works

How to buy a spa gift card online

  1. Open the gift card page. Go to omorfiabeauty.nyc/gift-cards and tap “Buy a Gift Card”.
  2. Pick an amount. Choose ⁠$50, ⁠$100, ⁠$250 or ⁠$500 — or tap Custom Amount and type any amount from ⁠$50 to ⁠$500.
  3. Add it to your cart. Tap “Add”.
  4. Review your cart. Tap “Next”. You can pay over time with Affirm if you like.
  5. Enter your phone or email. Then follow the checkout to pay.
  6. Get it by email. Your gift card is emailed to you — forward it, print it, or tuck it in a card.
