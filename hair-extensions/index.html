Four methods, one honest consultation

K-Tip ($175+ cash/debit · $181.13+ credit): keratin-bonded strands for the most seamless, long-wearing result. I-Tip ($150+ cash/debit · $155.25+ credit): bead-set strands with no heat and no glue — gentle on fine hair. Tape-In ($125+ cash/debit · $129.38+ credit): fast, flat, comfortable panels, ideal for a first set. Which one is right depends on your hair's density, your lifestyle and how much maintenance you'll actually do. That's what the complimentary extension consultation is for: we match the method, length and color before you commit to anything.

Living with extensions

Extensions need their own care — a dedicated brush, sleeping in a loose braid, and professional removal when the time comes. We offer an extension blowdry ($70+ cash/debit · $72.45+ credit) styled around your set and safe extension removal ($100+ cash/debit · $103.50+ credit) that protects your natural hair. Move-ups and maintenance are scheduled with your specialist at install.

Extensions for the big day

Bridal updos, prom styles and event hair all photograph better with a little extra length and density. Book extensions a few weeks before a wedding or prom so they settle in before the trial.

Questions

Good to know

Which extension method is best?

It depends on your hair's density and your routine — tape-ins for speed and comfort, K-tips for the most seamless wear, I-tips for no heat or glue, wefts for maximum fullness. The free consultation settles it.

How long do extensions last?

Tape-ins and wefts are typically moved up every six to eight weeks; strand methods wear longer between maintenance. Your specialist sets the schedule at install.

Do prices include the hair?

Prices are starting points for installation; hair cost depends on length, color and the amount needed, and is quoted at your consultation.

Can extensions damage my hair?

Not when installed, maintained and removed professionally. Skipping maintenance or pulling them out at home is where damage happens — which is why removal is a service.

Do you color-match?

Yes — matching to your color (or to a planned balayage) is part of the consultation.
