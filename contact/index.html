Good to know

Is there parking?

Yes — we share the Adams Fairacre Farms parking lot, so there's plenty of free parking. We're the grey and white building right there in the lot.

Do you take walk-ins?

When the book allows, gladly — but booking online or calling ahead is the only way to guarantee a time.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens — just give us as much notice as you can so we can offer the time to another guest. Full policy details are shared when you book.
