What microblading is

Microblading is a semi-permanent brow technique: a hand tool deposits pigment in fine, hair-like strokes along the brow to recreate the look of natural hair. It's the answer for sparse, over-plucked or uneven brows, and for anyone tired of drawing them on every morning. Results typically last twelve to eighteen months, with a fill recommended to keep the strokes crisp.

Your appointment starts with mapping: shape, arch and pigment are agreed on paper and on your face before any work begins. Expect the color to look darker for the first week and soften as it heals.

Questions

Good to know

How long does microblading last?

Twelve to eighteen months for most guests, with a fill ($250 cash/debit · $257.50 credit) recommended to keep the strokes crisp.

Does microblading hurt?

Most guests describe it as scratching or mild pressure; a topical numbing is applied before the work.

What's the healing like?

Brows look darker and bolder for about a week, then soften to the final shade over a few weeks. Keep them dry and skip makeup on the area as instructed.

Who shouldn't get microblading?

Pregnant or nursing guests and anyone with certain skin or medical conditions should wait; tell us your history when you book.

Is a touch-up included?

Fills are booked as a separate service ($250 cash/debit · $257.50 credit) when the strokes begin to soften.
