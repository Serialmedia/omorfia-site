Questions

Good to know

How often do lash extensions need fills?

Every two to three weeks keeps a set full — that's why fills are priced at both marks.

Classic or hybrid?

Classic is one extension per natural lash for an everyday look; hybrid mixes in volume fans for noticeably fuller, still-natural lashes.

How long does microblading last?

Twelve to eighteen months for most guests, with a fill recommended to keep the strokes crisp.
