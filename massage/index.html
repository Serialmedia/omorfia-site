Questions

Good to know

How should I prepare for a massage?

Arrive a few minutes early, hydrate, and tell your therapist about problem areas and pressure preference — then let them work.

Do I need to choose Swedish or deep tissue when I book?

No — you book the time, not the type. Choose Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone or a blend with your therapist when you arrive, and change your mind on the table if you like. Same price for any style.

Which length should I book?

60 minutes handles full-body maintenance; 90 gives real focus time on problem areas; 120 is the full reset. Whatever you pick, the style is chosen when you're here.

Can I get a massage on a Sunday?

Yes — we're open seven days a week, Sundays included, and Sunday massages are a guest favorite. Book online to lock in your time.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes — massage is our most-gifted service. Grab one on our gift cards page or at the front desk.
