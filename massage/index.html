Questions

Good to know

How should I prepare for a massage?

Arrive a few minutes early, hydrate, and tell your therapist about problem areas and pressure preference — then let them work.

Do I need to choose Swedish or deep tissue when I book?

No — you book the time, not the type. Choose Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone or a blend with your therapist when you arrive, and change your mind on the table if you like. Same price for any style.

Which length should I book?

60 minutes handles full-body maintenance; 90 gives real focus time on problem areas; 120 is the full reset. Whatever you pick, the style is chosen when you're here.

Can I get a massage on a Sunday?

Yes — we're open seven days a week, Sundays included, and Sunday massages are a guest favorite. Book online to lock in your time.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes — massage is our most-gifted service. Grab one on our gift cards page or at the front desk.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes — book two massages for the same time and you'll be treated side by side. For the full afternoon together, the For Two spa package adds facials and pedicures for both of you.

Are your massage therapists licensed?

Yes — every massage at Omorfia is given by a New York State licensed massage therapist. You can read about Debbie and Nadya on our team page and book either by name.

Where is Omorfia for massage?

751 Dutchess Turnpike (Route 44) in Poughkeepsie — the grey and white building that shares a parking lot with Adams Fairacre Farms, about five minutes from Vassar College and Arlington and ten from Marist. Free parking, private massage rooms.

Can I book a same-day massage?

Often, yes — we're open 9 AM to 8 PM seven days a week, so evening and Sunday slots are easier to find here than most places. Book online to see live availability.
